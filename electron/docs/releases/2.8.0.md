Hallym MIPS 2.8.0, the MIPS simulator for Hallym University's computer architecture labs. Your programs assemble, run and export exactly as in 2.7.1 (the same SPIM core, the same `.hmx` images). What changed is the screen you see when it opens.

## Download (Windows 64-bit)

One file: **`HallymMIPS-2.8.0-win-x64-setup.exe`**. Run it: it installs for you only (no administrator rights) and adds *Hallym MIPS* to the Start menu. If you have an earlier 2.x, it replaces it; the previous edition, 1.x ("Hallym MIPS Simulator"), is left as it is.

The program is not code-signed, so **Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" window the first time. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*** (Korean Windows: *추가 정보* → *실행*). Step by step: [한국어](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/blob/main/docs/usage/usage.ko.md#windows-경고-창-넘기기) · [English](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/blob/main/docs/usage/usage.en.md#getting-past-the-windows-warning).

## What you will see that is different

**The first screen is a circuit board the program draws.** Until 2.7.1 it was a video of the campus playing behind a white card. Now the program grows a board of its own: traces leaving a chip in the middle of the screen, cut at 45°, never crossing one another, each ending in a pad, in three depths with light around them. The whole board takes about eight and a half seconds to fill in, spreading outwards — the corners finish six seconds after the pins.

**The card is the chip.** The box with the two choices in it is the package in the middle of that board, with the pins of the drawn board on its edges. Inside it: the university's symbol, the program's name, and the two ways in — **바로 시작** first, **튜토리얼 보기** under it (they have swapped places). Both work from the moment they appear; there is no need to wait for the board.

**The name and the buttons carry the signal.** A light runs across the program's name, and round each button's border, as if the traces on the board carried on into the card. The first choice's light is brighter and comes more often than the second's.

**The campus video is gone** — the file as well, not just the screen. Nothing of it is in the installer any more.

If your PC has Windows' animation effects turned off, the finished screen comes up at once, with nothing moving — as before.

## Measured

Of the board at 1920 × 1080, over the screen with the card and the two bars left out:

| | 2.7.1 | 2.8.0 |
|---|---|---|
| mean brightness, of 255 | — (a video) | 51.4 |
| the board finished at | — | 8.5 s |
| the far corners after the pins | — | 6.0 s |
| drawing, while it grows | — | 0.80 ms a frame (median) |
| drawing, once settled | — | 0.20 ms a frame (median) |

Once the board has finished it is never drawn again: what keeps moving is a handful of pulses on a layer above it, and over half a minute of a settled screen nothing accumulates. Leaving the first screen takes all of it with it.

## Going back

If something is wrong with 2.8.0 on your PC, the earlier releases are still here:

- [2.7.1](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/releases/tag/v2.7.1): remove 2.8.0 first (Settings → Apps → Installed apps → *Hallym MIPS 2.8.0* → Uninstall), then install 2.7.1.
- [1.2.4](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/releases/tag/v1.2.4), the previous edition ("Hallym MIPS Simulator"): it installs beside 2.x, and the two do not touch each other.
