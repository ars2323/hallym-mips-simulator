Hallym MIPS 2.7.0, the MIPS simulator for Hallym University's computer architecture labs. Your programs assemble, run and export exactly as in 2.6.0 (the same SPIM core, the same `.hmx` images).

## Download (Windows 64-bit)

One file: **`HallymMIPS-2.7.0-win-x64-setup.exe`**. Run it: it installs for you only (no administrator rights) and adds *Hallym MIPS* to the Start menu. If you have an earlier 2.x, it replaces it; the previous edition, 1.x ("Hallym MIPS Simulator"), is left as it is.

The program is not code-signed, so **Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" window the first time. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*** (Korean Windows: *추가 정보* → *실행*). Step by step: [한국어](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/blob/main/docs/usage/usage.ko.md#windows-경고-창-넘기기) · [English](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/blob/main/docs/usage/usage.en.md#getting-past-the-windows-warning).

## What you will see that is different

- **The tutorial's card stands next to what it is talking about.** It sits right under the button or the line it points at (above it, or beside it, when there is no room). Before, a card about a toolbar button — Assemble, Step, Run, Reset — always stood in the middle under the title bar, whichever button it meant.
- **The tutorial has one more step, 21 in all.** At step 19, after an assembly error, the **4행으로 가기** button now takes you to a step that shows the line to fix: the cursor on it, the line marked red. Before, the button seemed to end the tutorial.
- **The title bar holds together with a big font.** With the font made bigger (Settings, or Ctrl+=), its buttons stay in one line, each with its border; in a narrow window they show as their icons (hold the mouse over one for its name) instead of running off the window.
- Your programs, and everything else in the window, work as in 2.6.0.

## Going back

If something is wrong with 2.7.0 on your PC, the earlier releases are still here:

- [2.6.0](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/releases/tag/v2.6.0): remove 2.7.0 first (Settings → Apps → Installed apps → *Hallym MIPS 2.7.0* → Uninstall), then install 2.6.0.
- [1.2.4](https://github.com/ars2323/hallym-mips-simulator/releases/tag/v1.2.4), the previous edition ("Hallym MIPS Simulator"): it installs beside 2.x, and the two do not touch each other.
