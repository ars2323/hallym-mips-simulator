The scanner compiled into the SPIM addon (from CPU/scanner.l) is generated
by flex (win_flex from WinFlexBison on Windows, flex 2.6).  flex's license
says that its scanner skeleton carries no copyright notice and that scanners
generated by flex are not bound by it, so nothing here is required; it is
kept as an acknowledgement of the tool the program is built with.

The parser (from CPU/parser.y) is generated by GNU Bison; Bison's special
exception places its output under the terms of this program's own license.
